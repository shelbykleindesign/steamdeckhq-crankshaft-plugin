How to play

  • Steer by turning the iPad like a steering wheel. The picture counter-rotates, so the horizon stays level with the real world.
  • Gas is automatic. Hold your left thumb on the screen to brake or reverse.
  • Drift: hold your right thumb while turning. Sparks go blue, orange, pink. Let go for a turbo.
  • Hit the yellow boost pads. Grass slows you down.
  • Online: one player taps Host and reads out the four-letter code; the other taps Join and types it in.
